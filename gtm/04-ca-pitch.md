# 04 — CA Channel Pitch

India has ~4 lakh practising Chartered Accountants. Each serves 50–200 MSME clients. An
MSME owner believes their CA and does not believe a sales call. **Ten CAs signed is
potentially 1,000 businesses of reach with no sales team.**

## 1. Why a CA actually wants this

Lead with their interest, not ours.

1. **43B(h) is already their headache.** Since FY 2023-24 they have been telling clients
   about it at every audit and have had **no practical tool** to act on it. At year-end they
   are the ones chasing the client's receivables data. We remove unpaid, unpleasant work.
2. **Their clients' working capital is their problem too.** A client short on cash delays
   the CA's own fees.
3. **₹1,500/client/month recurring** for a recommendation they would make anyway.
4. **It makes them look good.** A client who recovers ₹8 lakh remembers who suggested it.

## 2. The pitch (10 minutes)

### Opening
> Sir, main MSME receivables par kaam kar raha hoon. Aapke clients mein se zyadatar ka
> paisa 60-90 din atak jaata hai — sahi hai na?
>
> 43B(h) aap unhe batate honge. **Kitne clients ne actually use kiya hai?**

That last question does the work. The answer is almost always "bahut kam" or "koi nahi",
and they will explain why: nobody wants to send the letter, and drafting it is work.

### The problem, framed their way
> Exactly. Rule to hai, lekin koi use nahi karta — kyunki letter draft karna, interest
> calculate karna, aur phir buyer ke saath awkwardness. Client karta hi nahi.
>
> Nateeja: client ka paisa atka rehta hai, aur audit ke time aapko hi data chase karna
> padta hai.

### The product
> Maine ek system banaya hai jo ye poora kaam automatic karta hai:
>
> · Client ke Tally se outstanding uthata hai
> · Har buyer ko WhatsApp par follow-up bhejta hai — **client ke naam se**, Hindi ya
>   Punjabi mein
> · Reply samajhta hai — kisne date di, kisne dispute kiya
> · 45 din ke baad **43B(h) ka letter aur Section 16 ka interest calculation** draft kar
>   deta hai
> · **Bhejne se pehle client ki approval lega** — automatic legal notice kabhi nahi jaata

Say that last line clearly. It is the CA's first professional concern, and answering it
before they raise it is what earns the rest of the meeting.

### The ask
> Main abhi 10 CAs ke saath shuru kar raha hoon. **Aapko sirf itna karna hai — jin clients
> ka paisa atka hai, unko batana.**
>
> Har client par ₹1,500/month aapko milta hai, jab tak wo customer hai.
>
> Ek client se shuru karte hain — aap batao kaunsa, main khud onboard kar dunga, aap saath
> baith jaana.

**Always close on one client, never on a portfolio.** "Main sab clients ko bata dunga" is a
polite no. One named client with a date is a yes.

## 3. Commission structure

| Model | Terms | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Referral** | 25% recurring, lifetime of the account | Most CAs. Default offer |
| **White-label** | ₹1,500/client/mo flat; CA sets their own client price | CAs who want to package it into their own retainer |
| **Managed** | ₹999/client/mo + the CA charges their own service fee | CAs who want to run collections as a service line |

### Non-negotiables
- **Pay monthly, on time, visibly.** One late payment ends a CA relationship permanently,
  and they talk to each other.
- **Written agreement**, even for a handshake deal. They are accountants.
- **Recurring, not one-time.** A one-time referral fee buys one introduction; a recurring
  one buys an advocate.
- **Never go around them to their client.** Ever. This is the whole channel's trust basis.

## 4. Where to find them

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| **ICAI branch study circles** | ICAI has 5 regional councils and 170+ branches. Branches run monthly CPE seminars and study circles — the most accessible CA-networking channel there is. Contact the local branch office about a slot or a sponsorship |
| **ICAI conferences** | Regional and national conferences through the year. Good for volume, weak for depth |
| **Your own CA** | Start here. Ask for three introductions. Warm beats cold by a mile |
| **Pilot customers' CAs** | Ask each concierge customer: *"aapka CA kaun hai? Unse baat karwa do."* Already-warm, and they can see the result |
| **CA WhatsApp groups** | Every city has them. Join through a member, never cold-post |

**Best single move:** offer a **free 30-minute session at an ICAI branch study circle** on
*"43B(h) aur MSME receivables — practical implementation"*. It is genuinely useful content,
CPE-adjacent, and you are the only person in the room with a tool. Do not make it a product
demo — teach for 25 minutes, mention the tool for 5.

## 5. Sequencing

**Start with 10 CAs, not 100.** Pick ones with manufacturing-heavy client books inside your
chosen cluster.

| Phase | Target |
|---|---|
| Phase 0 (week 1–3) | **5 conversations**. Goal is learning: would you recommend this, at what commission, what worries you? |
| Phase 1–2 | 3 CAs actively referring, 1 client each onboarded with the CA present |
| Phase 3 (month 5–8) | 25 CA partners, ~200 businesses of reach |

Onboard the first client of every new CA **with the CA in the room**. They need to see it
work once. After that they will refer without you.

## 6. The 5 Phase-0 CA questions

These are discovery calls, not sales calls — same discipline as
[`01-call-script.md`](01-call-script.md).

1. Aapke kitne clients manufacturing mein hain, jinka paisa atakta hai?
2. 43B(h) par clients ko kya bolte ho? Kitno ne use kiya?
3. Agar aisa tool ho, aap recommend karoge? **Kya cheez rok sakti hai?**
4. Commission kaisa hona chahiye — recurring ya one-time? Kitna?
5. Aapke naam se jaana chahiye ya company ke naam se?

**Question 3's second half is the important one.** Common answers you should be ready for:

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| *"Client ka data third party ko doon, risk hai"* | DPA in writing, data stays in India, we are a processor not a fiduciary. Show them [`docs/09`](../docs/09-security-dpdp.md) |
| *"Agar galat legal notice chala gaya to meri reputation"* | Nothing legal sends without the client's per-invoice approval. Letters go on the client's letterhead, not ours. Show them the approval screen |
| *"Mere client ko tech nahi aati"* | Sab WhatsApp par hota hai. Koi login nahi. Onboarding main karta hoon |
| *"Buyer naraz ho gaya to client mujhe blame karega"* | Tone control, per-buyer limits, auto-stop on hostility. And you get the same digest the client gets |
| *"Ye to mera kaam kam kar dega"* | It removes unbilled work, not billed work. Year-end data chasing is unpaid |

Objection 2 is the one that decides the channel. Have the approval-gate screenshot ready
before the meeting — describing it is not the same as showing it.
