# 02 — Cluster & Outreach List

> **Verify before you dial.** Association office-bearers rotate annually and published
> numbers go stale. Every contact below should be re-confirmed from the association's own
> site or a visit. Do not cold-call a personal mobile you found in a directory — go through
> the association office.

## 1. Cluster priority

Own **one cluster completely** before starting the next. References travel inside a cluster
and almost never between them. 20 customers in Ludhiana is a defensible position; 20 spread
across five cities is nothing.

| # | Cluster | Sector | Why it's ranked here | Language |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Ludhiana, Punjab** | Bicycle parts, auto parts, engineering, hosiery | 1.5 lakh+ registered MSMEs. Dense, WhatsApp-native, severe receivables pain, strong association culture | Punjabi / Hindi |
| **2** | **Rajkot, Gujarat** | Auto components, machine tools, castings, bearings | Tight, well-mapped cluster (~160 auto-component MSME units: 80 micro, 65 small, 15 medium). Small enough to saturate | Gujarati |
| **3** | **Surat, Gujarat** | Synthetic textiles, polyester processing | Huge credit-sale volume, long payment chains through agents | Gujarati / Hindi |
| **4** | **Pune / Pimpri-Chinchwad, MH** | Auto components, engineering | Larger, more sophisticated buyers (Tata, Bajaj, M&M tiers). Higher ACV, longer sales cycle | Marathi / Hindi |
| **5** | **Tiruppur / Coimbatore, TN** | Knitwear, pumps, motors, foundry | Strong export orientation. **Needs Tamil templates — hold until week 13** ([roadmap](../docs/10-roadmap.md)) | Tamil |

**Start in Ludhiana.** Highest MSME density, Hindi/Punjabi works from day one, and the
association network is the most accessible of the five.

## 2. Where to find the businesses

### 2.1 Cluster associations ← best channel by far

A 20-minute slot at a monthly association meeting beats 200 cold calls.

| Body | Reach | How to approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Laghu Udyog Bharati (LUB)** | All-India, 250+ branches across 400+ districts, since 1994 · `lubindia.com` | The single best entry point. Find the local branch, attend a meeting as a guest, ask for a 15-minute research slot |
| **FISME** | Federation of Indian Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises — national | Policy-facing; good for credibility and intros, weaker for direct leads |
| **Ludhiana Cycle & Parts Manufacturers Association (LCPMA)** | Ludhiana cycle & parts · office at Focal Point Phase VIII, Mangli | Verify current office-bearers via the association office before contacting |
| Local sector bodies | Per cluster — auto parts, hosiery, textile processors, foundry associations | Ask LUB's branch secretary which ones matter locally. They will tell you in one call |

**How to ask for the slot:**
> *"Main MSME receivables par research kar raha hoon — 43B(h) aur delayed payments par.
> Kya main next meeting mein 15 minute members se baat kar sakta hoon? Findings association
> ke saath share kar dunga."*

Offering the findings back is what gets you in. Honour it — write a one-page summary and
send it.

### 2.2 Government / open data (free, structured)

| Source | What you get |
|---|---|
| **`data.gov.in`** — Udyam registration datasets | District-level counts of registered manufacturing MSMEs (7.83 crore enterprises registered as of Feb 2026). Use for **sizing and targeting a district**, not for individual contacts |
| **`dcmsme.gov.in`** — District Industrial Profiles (DIPs) | Per-district PDF: dominant sectors, unit counts, cluster descriptions. Read Ludhiana's before your first call |
| **`sameeeksha.org`** — cluster profiles | Detailed per-cluster manuals incl. unit counts and composition. Where the Rajkot numbers above come from |
| **`samadhaan.msme.gov.in`** | The delayed-payment filing portal. Study the process — you will be drafting applications for it |

### 2.3 Commercial directories

- **IndiaMART** — filter by city + category. Good top-of-funnel volume, low conversion.
  Best used to build a dial list *after* you have an association referral to name-drop.
- **Justdial / TradeIndia** — same, thinner.
- **GST portal search** — verify a GSTIN you already have; not a discovery tool.

## 3. Qualifying filter

Before spending a call, check:

```
✓ Manufacturer or B2B supplier (not retail, not B2C, not services)
✓ Turnover roughly ₹1–50 Cr
✓ Sells on credit terms — not advance-payment only
✓ Udyam registered (needed for the 43B(h) lever)
✓ 20+ recurring buyers
```

**Disqualify fast:** advance-payment-only businesses, pure exporters (different payment
mechanics), and anyone under ~₹1 Cr (the pain is real but the budget isn't).

Ending a bad call in 3 minutes is a skill. *"Samajh gaya — aapka model alag hai, aapka time
liya iske liye shukriya."*

## 5. If Ludhiana doesn't work

Do not immediately conclude the idea is dead. Diagnose in this order:

1. **Couldn't get 20 conversations** → a distribution problem, not a product problem. Fix
   the association route before anything else.
2. **20 conversations, nobody has the pain** → wrong cluster. Try Rajkot; it is tighter and
   more homogeneous.
3. **Pain is real, nobody pays ₹2,500** → wrong price, wrong buyer within the firm (you may
   be talking to the accountant, not the owner), or the concierge offer isn't concrete
   enough. Re-read [03-concierge-playbook.md](03-concierge-playbook.md) §1.
4. **Pain is real, they already solve it well** → the honest kill signal. Take it seriously.

Only #4 kills the idea. The other three are fixable, and the difference between them is why
you log every call.
